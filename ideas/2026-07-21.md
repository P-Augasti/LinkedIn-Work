## LinkedIn Post Ideas

1. **Hook:** "We obsess over onboarding. But most employees leave because of what happens in month 13."
   **Angle:** The "second-year slump" — after onboarding ends, recognition and engagement often drop sharply. Position this as a systems problem, not a management failure. Share a 3-point framework for sustained recognition beyond the first year. Ties directly to Vantage Circle's core product value and positions Puja as a practitioner-thinker, not just a brand voice.

2. **Hook:** "I used to think PR meant press releases. Here's what it actually took to build a global communications function from India."
   **Angle:** A candid, behind-the-scenes narrative on building the comms function at a B2B SaaS company headquartered outside a Tier-1 city. Themes: operating without a big-agency budget, earning credibility in international markets, and what "punching above your weight" really looks like. Authentic personal branding that doubles as a talent magnet for Vantage Circle.

3. **Hook:** "The best employee engagement programs I've seen don't start with HR. They start with the CFO."
   **Angle:** A counterintuitive take on how engagement ROI needs to be framed in finance language to get budget and executive sponsorship. Draw on real patterns from HR Tech conversations. Targets HR leaders, CHROs, and business leaders — broadens Puja's audience beyond the PR/comms niche and signals cross-functional strategic thinking.

## Personal Branding Ideas

1. **Start a "Comms Decoded" LinkedIn newsletter or mini-series:** A monthly or bi-weekly series where Puja breaks down one real corporate communications decision — a campaign, a crisis response, a media pitch — into its strategic components. Each post is short, specific, and practitioner-grade. This builds a distinct content identity that separates her from generic "thought leader" content, attracts a following among junior comms professionals (who become advocates), and creates a steady pipeline of speaking and guest-writing opportunities. The series title becomes her brand handle.

2. **Apply for the "40 Under 40" or equivalent HR/Comms industry awards in 2026:** Target lists: People Matters 40 Under 40, Campaign India's Women Leading Change, Economic Times Women Ahead, or PRCI (Public Relations Council of India) awards for communications excellence. Most have Q3 nomination windows. The act of preparing a nomination forces a useful audit of achievements, creates content fodder, and the shortlist/win generates earned media that would be difficult to get otherwise. Identify two to apply for and draft the submission in the next 30 days.
