1. **Hook:** "I used to think 'being strategic' meant knowing all the answers. It doesn't. It means asking better questions than everyone else in the room."
   **Angle:** Reframing what strategic thinking actually looks like in corporate communications — moving from reactive press releases to proactively shaping narratives. Share a real example of when a question (not an answer) changed the direction of a campaign.

2. **Hook:** "Our employee recognition program hit 92% participation this quarter. Here's the one thing we changed that most companies overlook."

3. **Hook:** "Nobody talks about the loneliness of being the only woman in the leadership meeting. I'm talking about it."

Tie it to the importance of visible representation in HR Tech. High-engagement format: short punchy paragraphs, ends with an open question to the audience.

This positions Puja as a communications analyst and educator, not just a practitioner, which is the content profile that attracts Director/VP-level peer engagement and recruiter attention.

Deadline research is needed, but the nominations cycle typically opens Aug–Oct for most Indian business publications. A shortlist or win creates earned media that is owned indefinitely on your LinkedIn and resume, and signals VP-readiness to executive search firms.
